Privacy Policy

How ASGT India collects, uses, and protects your personal information.

Last updated: November 2026  ·  Applies to: asglobaltrade.in

1. Who We Are

ASGT India (Advanced Supply · Global Transit) is a registered export trading company based in Ahmednagar, Maharashtra, India (Udyam: UDYAM-MH-01-0300536). We operate the website asglobaltrade.in and handle the personal data you submit through it.

For any privacy-related questions, contact us at info@asglobaltrade.in.

2. Data We Collect

We collect only the information you voluntarily provide when submitting an enquiry through our website forms. This may include:

  • Full name and company name
  • Email address
  • Mobile or WhatsApp number
  • Country
  • Shipping destination and quantity requirements
  • Certification and documentation requirements
  • Any additional notes you include

We do not collect payment information, browsing data beyond standard server logs, or any special categories of personal data.

3. How We Use Your Data

Your information is used solely to respond to your enquiry. Specifically:

  • To prepare and send you a quotation or product information
  • To arrange sample dispatch for qualified buyers
  • To follow up on your order or question

We do not use your data for marketing, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to your enquiry. We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties for their own use.

4. Form Submission Service

Our website enquiry forms are processed by FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), a third-party email delivery service. When you submit a form, your data passes through FormSubmit’s servers to deliver it to our inbox. FormSubmit does not store or use your data beyond delivery.

Please review FormSubmit’s Privacy Policy for details of their data handling.

5. Data Retention

We retain your enquiry details for as long as necessary to manage the business relationship, typically for the duration of any active trade discussions plus a reasonable period afterwards. If you would like your data removed, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Correction — ask us to correct inaccurate data
  • Deletion — ask us to delete your data
  • Objection — object to how we use your data

Buyers in the United Kingdom and European Union also have rights under the UK GDPR and EU GDPR respectively. To exercise any of these rights, email us at info@asglobaltrade.in and we will respond within 30 days.

7. Cookies and Tracking

This website does not use tracking cookies, analytics scripts, or advertising pixels. No third-party tracking is embedded on any page. Standard server access logs (IP address, page visited, timestamp) are retained by our web host for security and diagnostic purposes only.

8. Security

All pages on this website are served over HTTPS. We handle your enquiry data with reasonable care and limit access to those who need it to respond to you.

9. Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we will update the “Last updated” date at the top of this page. Continued use of the website after changes constitutes acceptance.
