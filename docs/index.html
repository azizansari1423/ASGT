Our Sourcing Advantage

India's Finest Onion-Growing Belt

Maharashtra's Nashik and Lasalgaon districts account for the majority of India's dehydrated onion production. We source directly from processors in this belt.

Nashik District

Maharashtra's primary onion-growing region. High-quality Kharif and Rabi crop. Known for optimal pungency and dry matter content — ideal for dehydration.

Lasalgaon Mandi

Asia's largest onion wholesale market. We monitor prices regularly to ensure competitive and stable supply pricing for our buyers.

Verified Processors

All suppliers are physically visited and scored on quality, documentation, capacity, and export readiness before selection.
