About ASGT India

India’s Dedicated Onion Powder Export Partner

ASGT India - Advanced Supply · Global Transit - is a registered export trading company based in Ahmednagar, Maharashtra. We specialise in sourcing, quality verification, and export of dehydrated onion powder to international importers, food manufacturers, and distributors.

As a focused trading company, we act as a dedicated bridge between vetted Indian processors and global buyers. We work with processors from the Nashik–Lasalgaon belt selected for quality, documentation readiness, pricing stability, and export capability. We handle full export documentation, eliminating the complexity and uncertainty often faced by international buyers when sourcing from India.

Registered under India’s Udyam MSME scheme and operating with one clear mandate: deliver export-grade onion powder with full transparency, correct documentation, and reliable supply timelines.
