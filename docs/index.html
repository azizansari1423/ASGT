About ASGT India

India-Based Export Trading Company

ASGT India (Advanced Supply · Global Transit) is a registered export trading company based in Ahmednagar, Maharashtra, India. We specialise in sourcing, quality verification, and export of dehydrated onion powder to international importers, food manufacturers, and distributors.

We act as a dedicated export partner — bridging verified Indian processors with global buyers who require consistent quality, complete export documentation, and reliable supply timelines. Every supplier in our network is physically visited and scored before selection.

Registered under the Government of India’s Udyam MSME scheme (UDYAM-MH-01-0300536). Goods are dispatched FOB Mumbai (JNPT), with full export documentation managed in-house.

Our Sourcing Advantage

India’s Finest Onion-Growing Belt

Maharashtra’s Nashik and Lasalgaon districts account for the majority of India’s dehydrated onion production. We source directly from verified processors in this belt.

Nashik District

Maharashtra’s primary onion-growing region. High-quality Kharif and Rabi crop. Known for optimal pungency and dry matter content — ideal for dehydration processing.

Lasalgaon Mandi

Asia’s largest onion wholesale market. We monitor prices regularly to ensure competitive and stable supply pricing for our international buyers.

Verified Processors

All suppliers are physically visited and scored on quality, documentation capability, production capacity, and export readiness before selection.
